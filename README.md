# *Call Me by Your Name*

**Director:** Luca Guadagnino

**Year / Setting:** 2017 — summer of 1983, northern Italy

A sunlit, slow-burn portrait of first love and its fallout, *Call Me by Your Name* traces 17-year-old Elio (Timothée Chalamet) as he falls hard for Oliver (Armie Hammer), his father's magnetic research assistant. The film trades plot for mood: lush cinematography, patient pacing, and a score that lingers like aftertaste, all working to make longing feel tactile. It’s less a tidy romance than an elegy for the way certain summers and people shape who we become.
